How I built this

Collect. BTRC publishes one month at a time, and older months are hard to find on its site. I took each month from the regulator's figures as reported by the Financial Express, The Business Standard, The Daily Star, Prothom Alo, Ittefaq and AMTOB, and noted the source for every row.

Validate. For every month with an operator split, the four operators must add up to the published total. All 19 such months match within rounding. Where two outlets disagreed, the sum check decided: one outlet had swapped Robi's August and September 2025 figures.

Gaps. No operator split was found for October and November 2024, January to March 2025 and April 2026, and some early months have no internet figures. They are left blank rather than estimated, and lines break across them.

Analyse. The CSV loads into SQLite. The queries compute market share, two-year change, month-on-month net adds with a window function that skips gaps, and period comparisons. Charts are drawn in plain JavaScript from the same numbers.

Precision. Months reported in crore are only exact to 0.1 million. A subscription is a biometric-verified SIM with activity in the last 90 days, so one person can hold several.

Disclosure. I work at Robi Axiata. This project uses only public regulator data and makes no use of internal figures.
