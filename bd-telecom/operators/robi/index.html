RRobiরবি

Robi had 59.04 million active subscriptions in August 2026, 30.9% of the market. Over two years it gained 0.68 million (+1.2%). Its lowest month in the data was April 2025 (56.60 million) and its highest was August 2026 (59.04 million). During the SIM-limit clean-up from July to December 2025 it changed by −0.14 million, and from February to August 2026 by +1.74 million.
