PDF Squeeze → scan, shrink and merge PDFs by Imtiaz

Developed by Mohd Imtiaz KarimDirect Channel Manager, Robi Axiata PLC

Scan documents with your camera or photos from your gallery, crop and clean them up, preview the result, then save as PDF or JPEG. The second tool compresses and merges PDFs you already have.

Everything runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

    Export

    For ID card layout, add the front first and the back second. Every two pages share one sheet at true card size, ready to print. Several pages saved as JPEG come in one ZIP.

    Drop PDFs here or tap to choose Select as many as you like. They are processed one after another.
    Compression level

    Balanced, Strong and Maximum redraw each page as an image. That gives big savings on scans but makes text unselectable. Use Light when the text must stay searchable. If a result comes out larger, the original is kept.
